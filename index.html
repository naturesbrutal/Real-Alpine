Ingredient Deep Dive

How Does
Alpine Reset Work?

Every ingredient in Alpine Reset is clinically dosed for real results — not marketing window dressing. Here's exactly how each one works.

01
Electrolyte Complex
3x vs. leading sports drinks

Alcohol is a diuretic — for every drink you have, your kidneys excrete significantly more water than you consume. This rapid fluid loss carries electrolytes with it: sodium, potassium, magnesium, and chloride. The resulting electrolyte imbalance is directly responsible for headaches, muscle cramps, and that bone-tired feeling. Alpine Reset's electrolyte complex delivers clinical doses of all four key electrolytes, rehydrating you at the cellular level.

02
Dihydromyricetin (DHM)
400mg — clinically studied

DHM is derived from Japanese raisin tree extract and is the most extensively researched anti-hangover compound in existence. Multiple randomized controlled trials show it significantly accelerates the clearance of acetaldehyde — the toxic metabolite of alcohol that causes most hangover symptoms. DHM also activates GABA receptors to reduce alcohol-related anxiety and supports the liver enzymes responsible for alcohol breakdown.

03
B Vitamin Complex (B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, B12)
Full spectrum · bioavailable forms

Alcohol aggressively depletes B vitamins — particularly B1 (thiamine), B6, and B12. These vitamins are essential cofactors in energy metabolism, neurological function, and red blood cell production. Severe B vitamin depletion is why hangovers make you feel mentally foggy, physically drained, and irritable. Alpine Reset uses the most bioavailable forms (methylcobalamin for B12, P-5-P for B6) for maximum absorption.

04
Milk Thistle Extract (80% Silymarin)
300mg · standardized extract

Silymarin — the active compound in milk thistle — is one of the most well-documented hepatoprotective (liver-protecting) agents in natural medicine. It works by stabilizing cell membranes in liver cells, preventing toxin penetration, and stimulating the regeneration of damaged liver tissue. Our extract is standardized to 80% silymarin for consistent, reliable potency.

05
N-Acetyl Cysteine (NAC)
600mg · pharmaceutical grade

NAC is a precursor to glutathione — the body's master antioxidant. During alcohol metabolism, your liver burns through glutathione at an alarming rate to neutralize acetaldehyde. When glutathione is depleted, oxidative damage runs unchecked. NAC rapidly replenishes glutathione stores, protecting liver cells and accelerating the detoxification of alcohol byproducts.

06
Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)
1000mg · buffered form

Vitamin C is a powerful antioxidant that works in tandem with glutathione to neutralize free radicals generated during alcohol metabolism. It also regenerates oxidized vitamin E, creating a synergistic antioxidant cascade. Beyond its antioxidant role, Vitamin C supports immune function (which alcohol suppresses) and aids in collagen synthesis, supporting tissue repair.

07
Ginger Root Extract
500mg · 5% gingerols

Ginger is one of the most well-studied natural remedies for nausea and GI distress. Its active compounds — gingerols and shogaols — accelerate gastric emptying, reducing the time irritants sit in your stomach, and have direct anti-nausea effects on the gut-brain axis. Multiple clinical trials confirm its efficacy for nausea relief. Alpine Reset uses a 5% gingerol standardized extract for consistent, reliable potency.

08
L-Theanine
200mg · pure form

L-Theanine is an amino acid found in green tea that promotes relaxed alertness by increasing alpha brain waves. Alcohol disrupts GABA and glutamate signaling, causing rebound anxiety and mental fog the morning after. L-Theanine helps rebalance these neurotransmitter systems, promoting calm focus without sedation — so you can actually think clearly and feel like a functioning human being again.
