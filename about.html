About Us

Alpine Nutrition LLC

I'm Charlie Kaludis, the Co-Founder of Alpine Nutrition.

I'm a D1 football player at Princeton, and my co-founder is a cross country runner. We come from completely different ends of the athletic spectrum, but we share the same standard when it comes to taking care of our bodies.

We're also college students. We wanted to enjoy the full college experience — going out, having fun, drinking — but without compromising our athletic performance and recovery. That's why we created Alpine Reset.

All the traditional hangover remedies we tried felt like they weren't sufficient for people who care about performance. Nothing was built for athletes who wanted both: a social life and a high standard of recovery.

So we built it ourselves.

Alpine Reset is our answer. Our proprietary formula is designed to help you recover faster, stay sharp, and keep performing without sacrificing the moments you actually want to enjoy.

We're just getting started. But the message is simple: don't compromise.

Charlie Kaludis
Co-Founder, Alpine Nutrition
